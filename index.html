Hi, I’m Jordan — a London-based 3D Designer who grew up with two big sources of inspiration: gaming and anime. What started as simply being fascinated by the worlds and characters I saw on screen eventually turned into a passion for creating those worlds myself.

Today, I specialise in 3D environments, products, and game-ready assets using Blender. With a BSc and MA in Computer Animation, I’ve developed a balance between technical precision and creative storytelling, allowing me to turn ideas into detailed, immersive visuals.

Over the years, I’ve worked across music videos, advertising, digital applications, and product visualisation, collaborating with producers, designers, and animation teams to bring concepts from an initial idea to a finished piece. I’ve also had the opportunity to mentor junior artists and help develop workflows that make the creative process more efficient.

For me, 3D is about more than just modelling. It’s about taking an idea that exists only in someone’s imagination and giving it form, atmosphere, and personality.

I create things I would have loved to see growing up — and that’s what keeps me creating.

Got an idea that only exists in your head?

Let’s give it form, atmosphere and personality.
